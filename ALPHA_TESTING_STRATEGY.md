# ANTHER ALPHA TESTING STRATEGY FOR BUSINESS IMPACT

## THE CORE VALUE HYPOTHESIS TO TEST

**Question:** Does Anther help musicians & entertainment professionals make better decisions about their music, discover collaborators, and navigate the creative landscape *faster and more accurately* than existing tools?

Current tools (Spotify, Apple Music, Shazam, genre tagging) answer "what category is this?" — Anther answers "what *sounds* like this?" That's the gap. The real test is whether that distinction matters enough to change behavior.

---

## ALPHA TESTING TIERS

### Tier 1: Discovery & Navigation (Easiest Signal)
**Goal:** Can musicians find musically relevant material they didn't know existed?

**Test Design:**
- **Cohort:** 15–20 musicians across 3–4 genres (not all the same style; you want variation in how well the map works across genres)
- **Protocol:**
  1. Give them a song (theirs or another artist's)
  2. Ask: "Using this tool, find 10 songs that sound similar to this"
  3. Compare their findings to: (a) what they find on Spotify's "fans also like," (b) what they find on SoundCloud discovery, (c) what a genre expert would say
  4. **Metric:** % of discoveries that are *new to them* AND *musically relevant* (measured via a listening test, not your judgment)

- **What you're measuring:**
  - Coverage: did it find things they didn't already know?
  - Relevance: were the matches actually musically similar (not just "lo-fi hip hop" because Spotify tags it that way)?
  - Serendipity: did it surface unexpected connections (e.g., a 90s alt-rock track and a modern R&B track that share harmonic DNA)?

- **Success bar:** >60% of discovered tracks should be new to them AND rated as "musically related" in a blind listening test

---

### Tier 2: Artist Development & A&R (Medium Difficulty)
**Goal:** Does Anther help artists understand their sound positioning and competitive landscape?

**Test Design:**
- **Cohort:** 10 independent/emerging artists + 3 label A&R scouts
- **Protocol:**
  1. Artists: upload 3 of their own tracks, get a map showing where they sit + nearest neighbors
  2. Ask: "Does this map match how you see your music positioning? What surprised you?"
  3. A&R scouts: use Anther to find 5 "new artists to watch" in a 30-min session
  4. Compare to their usual discovery method (SoundCloud deep-dives, playlist review, etc.)

- **What you're measuring:**
  - **Artist insights:** Do artists say "yes, that's my sound" or "no, that's way off"? (Measures whether the map is accurate to musician intuition)
  - **Positioning clarity:** Can artists articulate their lane better after seeing neighbors? (Measures whether it clarifies self-understanding)
  - **A&R efficiency:** How many "genuinely interesting" new artists did they find, and how long did it take?
  - **Competitive intelligence:** Did the neighbors reveal actual competitors or collaborators they didn't know about?

- **Success bar:** 
  - >70% of artists say the positioning is "accurate" or "makes sense with caveats"
  - A&R scouts find ≥3 artists per session they'd spend time on (vs. ~1 from their normal flow)

---

### Tier 3: Playlist & Content Curation (High Impact)
**Goal:** Does Anther speed up playlist building and improve playlist coherence for curators?

**Test Design:**
- **Cohort:** 8 independent playlist curators (Spotify, Apple, YouTube, or self-maintained playlists) + 2 label content managers
- **Protocol:**
  1. Task A: "Build a 30-track playlist on a theme you care about (e.g., 'introspective electronic')". Measure time + # of tracks you had to discard as "didn't fit."
  2. Do this twice: once with Anther, once with normal tools (Spotify's playlist builder, SoundCloud deep-dives, etc.)
  3. Task B: Have 20 listeners rate the coherence/flow of both playlists (blind, 1–10 scale)
  4. Task C: Ask curators "would you use this in your workflow?" and get specifics on where it fits

- **What you're measuring:**
  - Curation speed: time-to-30-tracks (Anther vs. baseline)
  - Playlist quality: listener ratings (perceived flow, coherence, discovery)
  - Fit-rate: % of Anther suggestions the curator kept vs. discarded
  - Workflow integration: would they actually use it, or is it a novelty?

- **Success bar:**
  - 20–30% faster curation with Anther (time per track down)
  - Listener ratings of Anther playlists ≥ listener ratings of baseline playlists
  - ≥75% of curator feedback says "yes, I'd use this regularly"

---

### Tier 4: Collaboration & Remix Discovery (Speculative but High-Value)
**Goal:** Does Anther help artists find collaborators and remix/sample sources?

**Test Design:**
- **Cohort:** 5 artists who actively remix or collaborate + 2 beatmakers who sample
- **Protocol:**
  1. Task: "You want to find a vocal artist to collaborate with / a sample source for a track. Use Anther to find 10 candidates."
  2. Ask: "How relevant were they? Would you actually reach out to any?"
  3. Compare to their usual methods (Twitter, SoundCloud follows, Bandcamp tags, etc.)

- **What you're measuring:**
  - Relevance of suggestions (do they actually sound like good collaborators?)
  - Actionability: would they actually contact these artists?
  - Speed of discovery
  - Serendipity: did it surface unexpected synergies?

- **Success bar:**
  - ≥3 out of 10 suggestions seem like "real collaboration potential"
  - ≥2 artists say they'd actually reach out

## KEY PERFORMANCE INDICATORS (KPIs)

### Tier 1 KPIs: Core Functionality
| KPI | Target | How to Measure |
|-----|--------|-----------------|
| **Discovery Novelty** | >60% of suggestions are new to user | Track checklist of "have you heard this before?" |
| **Musical Relevance** | >70% rated 7+/10 in blind listen test | Listening panel rates matches |
| **Genre Span** | Finds cross-genre matches when appropriate | Manual audit of whether boundaries are bridged |
| **Search-Result Stability** | Same query returns consistent top-10 | Repeat queries; check Rank Bieber Coefficient |

### Tier 2 KPIs: Value to Creatives
| KPI | Target | How to Measure |
|-----|--------|-----------------|
| **Positioning Accuracy** | >70% of artists say "yes, that's me or close" | Direct survey after map review |
| **Insight Generation** | >60% of artists say they learned something | Open-ended: "What surprised you?" |
| **A&R Discovery Efficiency** | ≥3 genuinely interesting artists per 30 min | A&R scouts count "would spend time on" artists |
| **Competitive Intelligence** | Artist can name ≥2 actual competitors from map | Follow-up: "Are these real competitors?" |

### Tier 3 KPIs: Workflow Adoption
| KPI | Target | How to Measure |
|-----|--------|-----------------|
| **Curation Speed** | 20–30% faster than baseline | Time trial: "Build a 30-track playlist" |
| **Playlist Quality** | Listener ratings ≥ baseline method | Blind 1–10 scale on coherence/flow |
| **Suggestion Keep-Rate** | >75% of Anther suggestions kept | Count kept ÷ suggested |
| **Workflow Integration** | >80% say they'd use regularly | Survey: "Would you add this to your process?" |

### Tier 4 KPIs: Business Impact (Harder to Measure)
| KPI | Target | How to Measure |
|-----|--------|-----------------|
| **Collaboration Leads** | ≥2 artists per session find "real potential" matches | Follow-up survey 2 weeks later: "Did you reach out?" |
| **Time-to-Insight** | 50% faster to understand music landscape | Qualitative + time trial |
| **NPS for Music Pros** | >40 among active users | Standard NPS survey (would you recommend?) |
| **Stickiness** | >50% of Tier 1 users return within 2 weeks | Login/session tracking |

## THE FEEDBACK FRAMEWORK FOR DEVS

**Don't just ask "did you like it?" — ask developers to obsess over:**

### 1. **Accuracy Questions** (Does the map match reality?)
- "Which suggestions surprised you (good or bad)?"
- "When did Anther *miss* — suggest something totally wrong?"
- "Are there any blind spots (genres/styles it doesn't handle well)?"
- Dev action: Identify weak spots in MERT or Leiden clustering

### 2. **Boundary Questions** (Does it respect music subcultures?)
- "Did it treat [your genre] fairly or did it lump you with something adjacent?"
- "Does the map respect the difference between X and Y [two related sounds]?"
- Developers need to know: does standardization/clustering resolution feel right?
- Dev action: Evaluate whether resolution needs tuning; check if certain genres degrade

### 3. **Determinism Questions** (Is the map stable/trustworthy?)
- "If I search for the same song twice, do I get the same neighbors?"
- "Does the map ever contradict itself (e.g., A→B neighbors, but B doesn't list A)?"
- Dev action: Check index stability, embedding reproducibility

### 4. **Practical Friction Questions** (Can they use it in real work?)
- "How long did it take to get a useful result?"
- "Did you have to retry/refine your query, or did it work first time?"
- "What would you need added to use this every day?"
- Dev action: Identify UX bottlenecks vs. algorithmic gaps

### 5. **Comparison Bites** (vs. what they already use)
- "Compared to Spotify's recommendations, what's different?"
- "Would you trust Anther more or less than [existing tool] for [specific task]?"
- "What does Anther do that [competitor] doesn't?"
- Dev action: Benchmark against baselines; identify unique value

### 6. **Failure Modes** (Where does it break down?)
- "Give me an example of a query where it totally failed"
- "Are there any songs/artists it can't handle?"
- "What's the weirdest/wrongest suggestion you got?"
- Dev action: Build test cases from failure examples

**Format:** 20-min structured interview (not free-form feedback) with each alpha user, recorded if possible. Same questions for all users so you can aggregate patterns.

## EXPERIMENTS TO RUN ALONGSIDE ALPHA TESTING

### Experiment 1: Map Accuracy Calibration
**Who:** 3 music experts (music journalist, producer, A&R scout)
- **Task:** Rate 50 random track pairs from the map as "musically similar" or "not"
- **Metric:** Precision/recall of the similarity index (does Anther agree with humans?)
- **Dev insight:** Validates whether standardization and resolution are in the right ballpark

### Experiment 2: Genre-Specific Deep Dive
**Who:** 5 artists in 1 specific genre (e.g., "experimental electronic" or "hip-hop")
- **Task:** Map 20 songs from their genre, audit the clustering
- **Metric:** Do artists recognize their sound neighbors? Are there surprise cross-genre bridges?
- **Dev insight:** Tests whether MERT + Leiden respects fine-grained genre distinctions without over-clustering

### Experiment 3: Benchmark Against Baselines
**Who:** Same 10 discovery-focused users from Tier 1
- **Protocol:** 
  - Session A: Find similar songs using Spotify + manual searching (30 min)
  - Session B: Find similar songs using Anther (30 min)
  - Compare results: overlap? quality? speed?
- **Metric:** Unique discoveries per session; quality (listening panel); time-to-result
- **Dev insight:** Where does Anther win vs. lose vs. existing tools?

### Experiment 4: Cold-Start Problem
**Who:** 5 artists new to Anther, 5 artists who've used it
- **Task:** Upload a brand-new, unreleased track; find neighbors
- **Metric:** Do they get useful matches? How does cold-start compare to established songs?
- **Dev insight:** Tests embedding generalization (can MERT handle out-of-distribution audio?)

### Experiment 5: Stability & Reproducibility
**Who:** Technical alpha testers
- **Protocol:** 
  - Query the same song 10 times over a week
  - Audit: are top-10 results identical? Rank order stable?
  - Rehost the corpus; do results change?
- **Metric:** Rank stability, embedding reproducibility
- **Dev insight:** Validates Leiden/index determinism

## INTERVIEW GUIDES BY ROLE

### For Artists
```
1. Discovery & Positioning
   - "Upload your track. Looking at where it sits, does that match how you see your sound?"
   - "What artists in the neighborhood surprised you? Any you didn't expect?"
   - "Are there any artists you'd want to be closer to?"

2. Workflow Integration
   - "Where could you see this fitting into your normal creative process?"
   - "What would you need from this tool to use it regularly?"
   - "vs. Spotify's algorithm, what's better/worse?"

3. Action & Outcome
   - "Based on this map, would you reach out to any of these artists?"
   - "Did anything here change how you think about your music?"
```

### For A&R / Label
```
1. Efficiency
   - "Using this tool, find 5 interesting new artists. How long did it take?"
   - "vs. your normal discovery method, how does this compare?"

2. Signal Quality
   - "Of the artists you found, how many would you actually spend time on?"
   - "Did any artists seem like 'breakout' candidates?"
   - "Any obvious duds or misses?"

3. Market Fit
   - "Could this become part of your regular workflow?"
   - "What would need to change for you to use it 3x/week?"
   - "Any competitive advantage vs. what you currently do?"
```

### For Curators
```
1. Playlist Building
   - "Build a 30-track playlist using this tool. How long? How hard?"
   - "vs. [their usual method], how does this feel?"
   - "What % of suggestions did you keep?"

2. Quality
   - "Listen to this playlist you built. Does it flow? Does it feel coherent?"
   - "Would you ship this playlist, or does it need tweaking?"

3. Adoption
   - "Could you see yourself using this for your regular playlists?"
   - "What would make it a must-use tool vs. 'nice to have'?"
```

## WHAT SUCCESS LOOKS LIKE (FOR BETA RECRUITMENT)

**You're ready to recruit for beta if:**

✅ **Discovery value proven:** >60% of non-expert users find novel, relevant music they didn't know about

✅ **Positioning clarity:** >70% of artists/labels say the map accurately reflects their/their artists' sound

✅ **Speed advantage:** Curation or discovery is ≥20% faster than existing tools

✅ **Low friction:** >80% of users say they'd use it regularly with minimal changes

✅ **Competitive parity:** At least one use case (discovery, A&R, curation, collab) is meaningfully better than Spotify/SoundCloud/existing tools

✅ **No major blockers:** No structural algorithmic failures (e.g., entire genres broken, cold-start unusable)

**Red flags (don't scale to beta yet):**
- <50% novelty/relevance in discovery
- Artists say the positioning is "way off"
- Speed is slower than existing tools
- >50% of users say they'd never use it
- Consistent failures on certain genres or track types
