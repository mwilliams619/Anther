How to use Anther

Add songs to the map

Build your own custom sound map by adding songs:

  • Search tracks: Find any song in the dataset or Spotify. Click a result to place it on the graph.
  • Load a playlist: Search for a playlist from the Million Playlist Dataset. All top tracks load as one cluster.
  • Upload audio: Drag & drop your own MP3, WAV, or FLAC files to analyze your unreleased music.

Explore the graph

  • Hover: See song details and similarity scores when hovering.
  • Click: Open the detail panel to see track metadata, related songs, and play the track.
  • Play a tour (⇉): Start playing the map like a playlist from that song, walking edges to similar tracks and jumping between disconnected islands.
  • Shift-click: Steer a tour that's already playing to that song, keeping everything played so far.
  • Drag: Reposition nodes to untangle crowded clusters.
  • Scroll: Zoom in and out to explore at different scales.

Filter and manage

The Map section lets you filter by artist, playlist, or album. Clear the map at any time to start fresh.

Want to see it in action?

Load the 2025 Year-End Top 20 — a chart-spanning mix of pop, hip-hop, and R&B hits to explore how music clusters by genre, vibe, and similarity.

Artist & genre discovery

Add a few songs from an artist or genre you like. Then use the Recommend similar button to show similar music you might not know. Hover edges to see similarity scores and find hidden gems.

Curation by similarity

Build a smooth musical journey through the map for DJing or sequencing. Identify outliers and remove them.

Music taste fingerprinting

Upload your favorite playlist or unreleased tracks. The emerging clusters and neighborhoods reveal patterns in your sound—what makes it you.

Get the most out of Anther

  • Start small: Add 5–20 songs first. Larger maps are harder to read but reveal richer neighborhoods.
  • Use filters: Filter by playlist or artist to zoom in on a specific subset of your map.
  • Recommend similar: When you're done exploring, get fresh corpus tracks that fit your sound.
